Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly to us. This includes your account email address and password (stored as a secure hash), the expense entries you create — including amount, category, date, and description — and any profile details you choose to add. We also collect basic technical information such as your browser type and IP address to keep the Service secure and functional.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data you provide to operate Spendly and deliver the features you expect: storing your expenses, generating summaries and breakdowns, and personalising your experience. Your financial data is used only to present your information back to you — we never analyse your spending for advertising, marketing, or any third-party purpose. Aggregated, non-identifying statistics may be used to improve the Service overall.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers with industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest. Account passwords are hashed using modern algorithms and are never stored in plaintext. We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may delete your account at any time from your profile settings, and we will permanently remove your associated records within a reasonable timeframe.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties for their own purposes. We may use a small number of trusted infrastructure providers — such as a hosting service and an email delivery provider — strictly to operate the Service on our behalf. These providers are bound by confidentiality and data-protection obligations and are not permitted to use your data for any other purpose.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. You can view and edit your expense entries at any time from within the app, and you can delete your account entirely through your profile settings. If you have any requests or questions about your data, please reach out using the contact information below.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us through the details provided on our website. We aim to respond to all privacy-related enquiries promptly.
